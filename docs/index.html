JevGAG — Jev-Grounded Adaptive Temporal Granularity for Causal Video Generation

Md Jisan Hossen · NUIST · 2026

Summary

JevGAG studies adaptive temporal granularity in causal video generation: when to generate one frame at a time (fine) versus several frames per chunk (coarse). It uses Jev 1.14 as an off-device, image-grounded System One oracle, adds a matched break-even law over hardware and operating point, a recovery + hysteresis controller, and a validated independent endpoint.
