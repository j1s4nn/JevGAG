\begin{abstract}
Adaptive temporal granularity for causal video generation requires three preconditions: a real
mode-dependent cost gap, a pre-generation signal, and an endpoint independent of the compared
modes. We audit all three with an off-device, image-grounded System One oracle (Jev 1.14).
We contribute (i) a matched break-even predictor over hardware and operating point, (ii) an
honest test of image-grounded versus text-grounded routing signals, and (iii) a validated
independent image-grounded endpoint. \emph{(results to be inserted)}
\end{abstract}
